# Savvy Wealth

> Savvy is a technology-driven, SEC-registered investment advisory firm (Savvy Advisors, Inc.) founded in 2021 and headquartered in New York, NY. The company combines licensed fiduciary financial advisors with proprietary technology — including Savvy Intelligence, an AI workspace for advisors — to deliver personalized wealth management for high-net-worth individuals and independent financial advisors. Savvy manages $5B+ in assets across 100+ advisors in 30+ U.S. locations.

Savvy serves two distinct audiences: **clients** seeking personalized wealth management and **financial advisors** looking to join or build a practice on Savvy's platform. All advisors operate as fiduciaries — legally obligated to act in clients' best interests — and custody assets at Fidelity or Charles Schwab. The company is backed by Thrive Capital, Industry Ventures, Canvas Ventures, and Vestigo, among others.
